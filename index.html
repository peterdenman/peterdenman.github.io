AnKing Sync settings, connection, first-time setup
First-time setup
  1. In Anki: Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons, enter code 2055492159 (AnkiConnect), then restart Anki.
  2. Keep Anki open and use this page in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on the same computer.
  3. Open this page with Anki running. The first time, Anki shows a permission popup for this site; click Yes. After that the page connects and syncs by itself every time you open it (and again if you come back after 15+ minutes). If the browser asks to let the page access devices on your local network, allow it.
  4. Still blocked? In Anki open Tools → Add-ons → AnkiConnect → Config and add your site's address (e.g. https://username.github.io) to webCorsOriginList, then restart Anki.
  5. For one-click AMBOSS practice, install the AMBOSS Anki add-on. The notebook's Start Qbank session button opens your weak cards in Anki's Browse window, where the add-on turns them into a matching Qbank session.
  6. After each analysis, results sync to GitHub, so your phone shows the latest snapshot even without Anki.

How it works
  1. On a lecture's row in Lectures, click Quiz (or pick the lecture under Lecture quizzes on this tab) and add the lecture slides, the practice quiz and the readiness quiz (PDF, PowerPoint .pptx or Word .docx). PDF works best: Gemini sees the images and tables. From PowerPoint, only the text and speaker notes are sent.
  2. Once all three are in, the page sends them with your prompt to Gemini and saves one quiz for each day in Quiz days after the lecture. The slides are the source; the two quizzes only set the style.
  3. The lecture's Quiz panel shows each quiz's status as it's written, with Open to check or take it early.
  4. On those days, Today shows a Lecture quiz row. At most Most quizzes per day show at once; the rest wait for the next day. A lecture's second quiz is written to test different facts than its first.
  5. The files stay on the computer you added them on; the questions and your scores sync, so you can take the quizzes on your phone.
  6. AI-written questions can have a wrong answer key. Each question names the slide it comes from, so you can check one that looks off.
  7. Protect your key: in Google Cloud Console, restrict it to the Gemini API and to your site's address.

Leeches

Cards Anki has tagged as leeches because you kept forgetting them (8 lapses by default, and Anki usually suspends them). Leeches cost far more time than they're worth, so fix the card before bringing it back: open it in Anki, reword it, add an image or mnemonic, or split it into smaller cards, then unsuspend it there.
